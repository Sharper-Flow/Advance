Q6 (complement vs replace): Every parallel system keeps a bounded-lifecycle container (Linear project/cycle, Jira epic, GitHub Projects, GitLab epic) SEPARATE from an unbounded product backlog, moving items between them by linking/promotion, not by relocating storage. The proposal's design — keep Epic-bound shells in Temporal, add a filesystem backlog as the un-parented pool, import via adv_epic_add_shell backlog_ref (one-way) / promote to change — is the industry-standard complement pattern. COMPLEMENT, do not replace. Bounded<->unbounded migration should be a linking/provenance operation, never a storage move that risks losing the Temporal-authoritative Epic entry.
